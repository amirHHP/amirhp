Last week I was at an event called “T-Startup” at the physical-education faculty of the University of Tehran, without knowing what was in it. I thought it was a small conference about startups, and that after a few two- or three-hour talks it would be over. To my surprise, when I arrived everyone was still registering. After a lot of asking I learned it was again the same pitch-an-idea-and-raise-money thing, at the end of which few people will give decent money to an unbuilt idea. That far was not so strange. I was shocked when I learned that [T-Startup](http://tstartup.ir/) is another “accelerator.” A few days earlier someone had tweeted a newly founded accelerator’s site and said they had never seen it. So in less than a month the number of accelerators in Iran reached five, and maybe there are more we do not know about.

![Startup](http://betanews.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/bubble_burst.jpg)
Let us go over a story together. From 1993, when Mosaic was born as the first graphical browser, internet companies sped up. The market got very hot. Anyone added an E or a dot-com to their name and then sold it well. A lot of profit-seeking investors, seeing an extraordinary future for internet companies, invested heavily. You could no longer tell real companies with potential from bubble companies. Everyone was investing in the internet, and honestly from 1995 to 2000 the internet businesses were doing very well. Internet companies got larger and larger until suddenly… boom, the dot-com bubble burst. From 2000 to 2002 the internet-tech market fell by five billion dollars, and a large number of companies vanished. WorldCom’s bankruptcy was at the time the third-largest in US history.

Something similar is happening to startups in Iran. Most of them also work in computing and IT, and in the past two years there was almost no event where startups formed in other fields, which might have let us say the growth was natural. On the surface this may look pleasant and profitable, but given how fast it has gone, the danger of this bubble bursting is high.

[![Startup](http://amirhp.ir/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/Screenshot-20.png)](http://amirhp.ir/wp-content/uploads/2015/03/Screenshot-20.png)
Now we have four accelerators, thousands of startups large and small, and — before I forget — Tehran is hosting [five Startup Weekends](http://tehran.startupweekend.ir/) for the first time in the world, at Amirkabir, Tehran, Sharif, Iran University of Science and Technology, and Shahid Beheshti. If seven startups come out of each, that is 35 startups in a single week.
